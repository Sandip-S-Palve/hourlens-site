Hourlens Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Hourlens is a personal time tracker. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app. In short: your data stays on your devices (and, only if you turn it on, in your own private iCloud), and we never collect, see, or share it.

Information we collect

None. Hourlens has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash-reporting services. The app does not connect to any server operated by us. The only online service it can use is Apple's iCloud, and only if you turn on iCloud sync.

Information stored on your device

The categories, time entries, plans and tasks you create are stored on your iPhone or iPad, inside the app's private storage. Hourlens's widgets read the same data through an App Group container on the same device so they can show your current timer. Unless you turn on iCloud sync (below) or export it, this data does not leave your device.

Apple Watch

If you use the Hourlens Apple Watch app, your iPhone sends it what is currently running and today's tasks, and the watch sends back the buttons you tap, directly between your own devices using Apple's WatchConnectivity. Nothing passes through any server of ours.

iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Sync with iCloud in Settings, your Hourlens data is stored in the private database of your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so your other devices signed into the same Apple ID can read it. Only your devices can read this data; we have no access to it. It is governed by Apple's privacy policy. Turning sync off stops it; you can delete Hourlens data from iCloud in iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Notifications

Hourlens can show a few local notifications that your device schedules itself: an evening reminder about paused sessions, and, if you turn on focus sessions, a nudge at the end of a focus block or break. If you use iCloud sync, iCloud may send silent notifications to tell the app that another of your devices changed something. No notification content is sent to us.

Exporting your data

If you tap Export as CSV in Settings, the app creates a file of your time entries and opens the standard iOS share sheet. Where that file goes (Mail, Files, AirDrop, etc.) is entirely your choice and is governed by the app you share it with.

Backups

If you have iCloud Backup or computer backups turned on, iOS may include Hourlens's data in those backups as it does for all apps. Those backups are managed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries and categories in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Hourlens does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Tracking

Hourlens does not track you across apps or websites and does not use the device's advertising identifier.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, for example if an optional sync feature is added, we will update this page and the date above before the change ships.
