Hourlens Support

Need help or found a bug? Email sandip1980@gmail.com and include your iOS version and what you were doing. I usually reply within two working days.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. See the privacy policy.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Hourlens and pick a size.

How do I back up or move my data?

Settings → Export as CSV saves your entries to Files, Mail or anywhere the share sheet offers.
